About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Minnesota (GPA 3.97/4.0), advised by Dr. Catherine Qi Zhao. My research focuses on machine learning systems that generalize across distributed, heterogeneous environments — with applications in biomedical signal processing, neural rehabilitation, and IoT networks.

My work spans federated learning, graph neural networks, and EMG-based gesture recognition, with the overarching goal of deploying interpretable and privacy-preserving AI in real clinical and engineering settings. I have published 16 journal papers in venues including IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, IEEE IoT Journal, and Journal of Neural Engineering, and 5 conference papers.

I am supported by the University of Minnesota Doctoral Dissertation Fellowship (2026) and collaborate with Fasikl Incorporated and Google DeepMind.
