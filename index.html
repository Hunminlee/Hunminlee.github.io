01 · About

I am a researcher and engineer with a focus on machine learning applications in biomedical signal processing and human-computer interaction. My work spans from designing graph-based representations of EMG signals to building federated learning frameworks for privacy-preserving model training.

I am particularly interested in developing AI systems that are both theoretically grounded and deployable in real-world settings — bridging the gap between academic research and practical engineering.
