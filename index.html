I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities, advised by Prof. Catherine Qi Zhao in the VIP Lab.

My research focuses on building machine learning systems that generalize across distributed, heterogeneous environments — with applications in biomedical signal processing, neural rehabilitation, and IoT networks. I design federated learning frameworks and graph neural network architectures that enable efficient, personalized, and privacy-preserving AI — particularly for EMG-based gesture recognition and upper-limb rehabilitation systems.

Before joining the Ph.D. program, I received an M.S. in Computer Science from Georgia State University (2022), where I was advised by Prof. Donghyun Kim and Prof. Yingshu Li, and a B.E. in Computer Engineering from Chonnam National University, South Korea (2020). From March 2015 to December 2016, I served in the Republic of Korea Army Special Duty Team (SDT).

I have published 16 journal papers in venues including IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, IEEE Transactions on Neural Systems and Rehabilitation Engineering, and Journal of Neural Engineering, along with 5 conference papers. I am currently collaborating with Fasikl Incorporated on AI-powered upper-limb neural rehabilitation and with Google DeepMind on federated learning for distributed networks.

Since 2021, I have been leading Seeking-SOTA, a deep learning study group that convenes weekly, bringing together researchers, academics, and professionals in South Korea dedicated to staying at the cutting edge of deep learning. Let me know if you are interested in joining!

My Ph.D. program is generously supported by the Doctoral Dissertation Fellowship from the University of Minnesota.
