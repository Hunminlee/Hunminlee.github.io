About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Minnesota (GPA 3.97/4.0), advised by Dr. Catherine Qi Zhao. I am a recipient of the Doctoral Dissertation Fellowship (2026), recognizing the university's most accomplished Ph.D. candidates.

My research focuses on building machine learning systems that generalize across distributed, heterogeneous environments — with applications in biomedical signal processing, neural rehabilitation, and IoT networks. I have published 16 journal papers in venues including IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, and Journal of Neural Engineering, along with 5 conference papers.

I am currently collaborating with Fasikl Incorporated on AI-powered upper-limb neural rehabilitation and with Google DeepMind on federated learning for distributed networks.
